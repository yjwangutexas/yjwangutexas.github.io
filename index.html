Yijie Wang

Assistant Professor

School of Economics and Management
Tongji University

I am an Assistant Professor in the School of Economics and Management at Tongji University. Previously, I was a quantitative researcher in the Equity Investment Department at CITIC Securities.

I received my Ph.D. in Operations Research and Industrial Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin in 2023, where I worked with Dr. Grani Hanasusanto.
